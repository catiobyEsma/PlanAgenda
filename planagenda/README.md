# PlanAgenda

PlanAgenda is a minimalist productivity application designed to help you keep track of your daily curriculums, exams, and extracurricular activities with timely reminders.

## Privacy Policy

* **Data Collection:** PlanAgenda does not collect, process, or share any personal data with third parties. 
* **Permissions:** The only permission requested by the application is notification permission. This permission is used solely to deliver timely reminders about your lessons, exams, and activities, and is technically required to fulfill the core service that the app promises to you.
* **Security:** No personal data is collected, transmitted to any server, or used for advertising or analytics purposes.
